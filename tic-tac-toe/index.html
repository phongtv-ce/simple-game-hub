Tic-Tac-Toe

Rules

  • Two players take turns: X goes first, then O.
  • Click an empty cell to place your mark.
  • Set N to play on an N x N board (default is 3).
  • Win by filling an entire line with your mark: a row, a column, or either main diagonal.
  • If every cell is filled and no one has won, the game is a draw.
